# Third-party assets

## Kenney Nature Kit (2.1) — GLB-ready redistribution

- **Creator:** Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- **Pack used:** `kenney_nature_kit_glb_cc0_v1` (Eclair Assets GLB redistribution, 329 models)
- **License:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) — free for personal, educational and commercial use; attribution not required but appreciated.
- **Full license text:** `vendor/kenney-nature-kit/License_Kenney.txt`
- **Usage in SKYFORGE:** a curated subset (32 models) is embedded base64 into
  `src/gfx/models.js` (generated by `tools/embed_glb.mjs`) and used for trees,
  rocks, logs, stumps, flowers, bushes, ferns, mushrooms and the campfire.
  Models stay inside the game bundle — no runtime network requests.

## All other content

Everything else in this repository is original procedural content
(code-generated meshes, shaders, audio) and is not third-party.
